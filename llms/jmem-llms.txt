# JMEM (Jmem Tek)

> Upstream semiconductor company specializing in Application-Specific Integrated Circuit (ASIC) solutions for quantum-resilient cybersecurity. Products include JRoot, JPUF, JCrypt, JShield and Zanker (a PUF-based post-quantum cryptography chip), delivering NIST-aligned quantum-safe encryption and hardware-anchored identity authentication. Portfolio company of 500 Global. No public API or developer surface is published at this time.
